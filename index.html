Frequently Asked Questions

Does CatalogVision AI overwrite my manual changes?

Never. Our publishing pipeline uses atomic compare-and-set verification. If a product attribute was edited manually in your Shopify Admin, the engine detects the digest mismatch, refuses to overwrite it, and preserves your manual changes.

How does rollback work if I don't like a batch?

Every batch write creates an immutable Change Journal entry storing prior values and digests. Clicking "1-Click Rollback" restores original values. If anyone manually modified an attribute after enrichment, our engine preserves their edit as a conflict.

Which apparel categories and attributes are supported?

V1 supports Tops, T-Shirts, Shirts, Blouses, Sweaters, and Hoodies. It targets four high-accuracy visual attributes: Primary Color, Pattern, Neckline, and Sleeve Length. Fabric composition and care instructions are parsed strictly from supplier text/CSVs rather than guessed from images.

Will this work with the Shopify Search & Discovery app?

Yes! CatalogVision AI writes directly to Shopify's standard taxonomy metafields (shopify--discovery--...), which are the exact source used by Shopify Search & Discovery for storefront filters.
